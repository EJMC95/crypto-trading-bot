# Snap Back — Lighter dislocation census log

Weekly evidence log for 🧲 Snap Back (`lighter_dislocation_bot.py`, service `snap-back-shadow`,
row `lighter-dislocation-lshadow`). Shadow-only; EVIDENCE, not P&L. Census floor 50bps,
tradeable bar 150bps net. GRADUATE gate: ≥30 joined shadow trades, positive both-halves modelled
P&L, exits mostly reconvergence. CLOSE gate has TWO arms — **A (STARVED)**: by ~2026-08-10 with <5
trades and ≥150bps gaps absent; **B (FALSIFIED, added 21-Jul)**: ≥15 closed trades over ≥10
independent episodes spanning ≥14d, reconvergence ≤1/3 of exits in BOTH halves, and pnl ≤ 0 ⇒ the
gaps are durable repricings that time out rather than snap back. Either way: do NOT lower the
threshold to revive it — under arm B a lower bar is strictly worse, since it admits more of the
non-converging gaps that produced the verdict. Managed by scheduled task `snap-back-census-check`
(Mondays 09:16 AEST).

An EPISODE, not a trade, is the unit of evidence for a convergence claim: a closed trade starts a
new episode unless it re-enters the SAME coin within 4h of the prior close on that coin. Six
consecutive re-entries into one unresolved gap say one thing about convergence, not six.
