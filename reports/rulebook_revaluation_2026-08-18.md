## B · Rules whose PREMISE died while the rule stood — optimised today `(px)`

The failure mode here is different: the rule was right when written and the
world moved. Two found, both on 🌾 carry, both shipped:

1. **`CARRY_MIN_VOL = $2M`** — justified 3-Aug by a point-in-time census
   ("$1M unlocks zero books today"). Over the full 34.9d tape the $1–2M band
   holds MORE of the cell's life than the ≥$2M band: occupancy 5.73% → 13.42%,
   coins 3 → 6, at $1M. The book sat flat 5.2 days at `eligible 0` while its
   own cage held the room. **Shipped $1M** (cage lo, registry synced,
   real-money floors untouched).
2. **`FLIP_GRACE_H = 1h`** — measured on this cell's own coins as churn
   (192/231 exits paying the RT on sign wobbles, h2 negative) vs 6h (+$41.17,
   t=2.96, both halves). The 6h fix was QUEUED on 13-Aug to protect carry's
   mid-window sample; that sample froze on 12-Aug and cannot update `(pf)`,
   so the wait protected nothing. **Shipped 6h** while the book is empty —
   one policy for the whole fresh sample.

The general lesson worth keeping: **a refusal recorded from a point-in-time
census decays; a refusal recorded from a tape-wide measurement does not.**
Where a rule cites "today's" census as its evidence, re-measure before
trusting it.
